Multi Persona Uploader is a personal tool used by the operator of the 맵핵트레이딩 (Maphack Trading) channel. It uploads videos that the operator made and approved to the operator's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. It does not access anyone else's accounts or data and is not offered as a service to the public. See the Privacy Policy.
